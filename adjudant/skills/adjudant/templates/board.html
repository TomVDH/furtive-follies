Drag an order between stages, saved to this browser instantly. On a touch screen, tap an order to pick it up and tap a stage to put it down. Hit connect file and pick board-data.json to auto-save to disk (Chromium). Card ids and mono ref · tags cross-link your codes (specs, handoffs, commits). Type in filter (or press a legend key) to narrow; Esc clears. Focus a ticket (Tab) and press [ / ] to move it a stage left / right. A column with "wip": N in board-data.json shows an over-limit count in red; "stamp", "stampTone" and "muted" set its terminal-lane treatment.
